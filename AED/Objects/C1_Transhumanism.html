Transhumanism and "Death as a Technical Bug"

Transhumanists like Aubrey de Grey or Ray Kurzweil argue that death is not a metaphysical necessity, but a series of biological problems (cellular senescence, protein cross-linking, etc.) that can be solved.

  • The Threat: If a student believes they are only 10 or 20 years away from Longevity Escape Velocity (the point where life expectancy increases by more than one year for every year that passes), your "teaspoon of death" might start to taste like "yesterday's news." They may stop seeking Nirvana and start seeking a software update.
  • The Counter: Even with radical life extension, the Second Law of Thermodynamics (entropy) remains. An "immortal" body can still be destroyed by a falling piano or a cosmic event.
