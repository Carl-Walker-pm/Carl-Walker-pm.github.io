Digital Immortality (Substrate Independence)

The idea of "Mind Uploading" suggests that the "I" is just information. If consciousness can be transferred to silicon, the biological death you teach becomes merely a "change of hardware."

  • The Threat:This destroys the concept of memento mori because it removes the "mori." If the "ego" can be backed up and restored, the urgency of dissolving the ego (Nirvana) vanishes.
  • The Counter:Is a copy of a person actually the person? This is the "Teleporter Paradox." You can argue that digital existence is a simulation of life, not life itself.
