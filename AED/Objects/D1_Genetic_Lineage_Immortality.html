The Philosophy of "Genetic/Lineage Immortality"

This is the biological argument that we do not die as long as our DNA or our impact continues.

  • The Concept:The individual is merely a temporary vessel for the "immortal" germline.
  • The Threat:If a student identifies deeply with their children, their works, or their contribution to the species, they may feel they have already achieved a form of immortality. They might see your "teaspoon of death" as a morbid obsession with the vessel rather than the content.
  • The Conflict:It replaces the internal pursuit of Nirvana (extinction of the self) with an external pursuit of Legacy (extension of the self).
