Quantum Immortality (The "Many-Worlds" Threat)

Based on certain interpretations of quantum mechanics, some argue that from the perspective of the observer, one can never actually experience their own death.

  • The Concept:If the universe splits at every decision, there is always a branch where you survive. Therefore, you are "immortal" from your own point of view because you only ever experience the timeline where you are alive.
  • The Threat:This can lead to a dangerous form of Solipsism. If a student believes they cannot die, the practice of "taking death by the teaspoon" feels like a hallucination. It removes the humility that death usually provides.
