Cosmic Conscious Continuity (The "Ocean" Theory)

This is the idea that consciousness is a fundamental property of the universe, and we are just "apertures" through which the universe looks at itself.

  • The Concept:Death is not a "letting go" or a "dissolving," because there was never an "I" to begin with—only the Cosmic Whole.
  • The Threat:While this sounds like Nirvana, it can be used to bypass the work of your curriculum. A student might say, "If I am the Universe, why do I need to practice death? I am already everything." It turns a disciplined practice into a passive intellectual comfort.
