How it works

Three steps. All human.

I.

The conversation

A private, unhurried one-on-one consultation. Who you are, what you're looking for, what you won't settle for. Nothing is written down without your word.

II.

The curation

I meet people. I listen. When someone fits what you described — truly fits — I make the introduction. One at a time. Never a catalog, never a feed.

III.

The refinement

After every introduction, we talk. What worked, what didn't, what surprised you. Each introduction sharper than the last — because I'm learning you.
