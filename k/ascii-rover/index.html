☾ Moon Rover

An ASCII game — dodge the crater rocks, collect the samples.




  
  
  



Keyboard: ← → or A / D. On the phone: tap ◀ ▶ (or tap the left / right half of the screen). Each ◆ sample is +10. Each rock costs a shield — you have 3. The Moon gets faster as you go.
