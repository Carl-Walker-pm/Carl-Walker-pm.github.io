The Project

A hedge fund is, before it is anything else, a machine for making decisions under uncertainty with other people's trust at stake. This project studies that machine from the ground up: how capital should be allocated, how risk should be priced, and how a decision should be recorded so that it can be judged later — by its reasoning, not by its luck.

The project is at the research stage. Its positions are theses; its results so far are documents, models and a discipline. When — and only when — the research supports it, the question of a working fund will be decided in the open, with the same written standard applied to that decision as to any other.

One sale of judgment, repeated carefully for a decade, is worth more than a hundred impulses. The unit of this project is the decade.

Principles

1 · Risk before returnThe first question of any position is what can be lost, and whether the loss can be borne. Return is the second question.
2 · First principles, not consensusThe crowd is not evidence. A thesis must stand on physics, economics and arithmetic — or it does not stand.
3 · Written theses onlyWhat is not written does not exist. Every position enters the ledger with its reasoning, its price and its exit — before it enters the market.
4 · Measure from the ledgerResults are read from records, not from memory or mood. Estimates are labeled as estimates; invented numbers are a firing offense.
5 · Patience is a positionCash held deliberately is a decision, not an absence of one. The project would rather miss a hundred moves than be forced into one.
6 · The decade is the unitCompounding is a long game played at long horizons. Short-term applause is not a metric this project reports to.

Research Directions

Macro Structure

How the large machine moves: rates, credit, energy, demography. Macro first, because individual securities are weather inside a climate.

Business Quality

Real operating businesses, studied as businesses — who is building toward durable value, and what that value is made of. The chronicle of people aiming at their first billion is field research for this desk.

Risk Systems

Position sizing, correlation under stress, and the failure modes that only appear when several things go wrong in the same week.

Scenario & Deal Analysis

A deal is an outcome, not a success. Negotiation explores the scenario space — tension, potential, and the cost of realization — and a deliberate no-deal can be the winning move.

  • No performance figures are published, because none are claimed. No returns are promised, implied, or projected on this page.
