Your journey

Five steps, one team

A typical IVF cycle takes four to six weeks from first consultation to pregnancy test. Here's how it unfolds at EVEA.

1

Consultation

Meet your doctor — in person or online. We listen first: your history, your hopes, your limits.

2

Diagnostics

A focused set of tests for both partners. Only what changes decisions — nothing for the sake of it.

3

Your protocol

A written plan with fixed pricing: stimulation, retrieval, laboratory work and transfer, step by step.

4

Transfer

Embryos are cultured and monitored in our own lab; the best one meets you on transfer day.

5

Support

The two-week wait is the hardest part. Your coordinator is a message away — and we stay with you after the test, either way.

FAQ

Questions everyone asks first

Is there an age limit?

Treatment with your own eggs is usually considered up to the mid-40s, depending on ovarian reserve and overall health; donor programs extend the options further. Your doctor will give you an honest picture at the first consultation — including when the honest answer is that chances are low.

How long does one cycle take?

From the start of stimulation to the pregnancy test: about four to six weeks. Diagnostics before that take one menstrual cycle in most cases. International patients can do much of the preparation from home, with two shorter visits.

Can I come as a single woman or in a same-sex couple?

Yes. Single women and female couples are welcome in our donor and IVF programs, with the legal side explained clearly before anything begins.

What if the first cycle doesn't work?

It happens, and it is nobody's failure. We review what the cycle taught us — embryo quality, response, timing — and adjust the protocol. Many families need more than one attempt; your pricing and your plan will always say so honestly, in advance.
