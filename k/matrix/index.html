The 36 × 36 — a map of every game that could exist

If the game industry were born today, genre would not be a camera or a setting. It would be two questions: what does the player do (axis X), and what kind of system answers (axis Y). 36 × 36 = 1,296 cells — 1,296 directions a game can take. Every tile below is a generated 16:9 cover, and every tile is a door: tap it, and the engine generates that game — first screen first, saved, never generated twice.

Axis X — what the player does
X01–06 Act & Move · X07–12 Think & Decide · X13–18 Create & Build · X19–24 Run Systems · X25–30 Talk & Influence · X31–36 Explore & Experience
Axis Y — what the world is
Y01–06 Space & Physics · Y07–12 Rules & Information · Y13–18 Living Systems · Y19–24 Society & Power · Y25–30 Economy & Production · Y31–36 Mind & Meaning

Rows are Y (the world), columns are X (the action). Scroll the map both ways. Example: X16 × Y17 — Programming × Artificial Minds: a game about programming and teaching a team of AI agents.
