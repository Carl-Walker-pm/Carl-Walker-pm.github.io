The Matrix 2 × 2

The taxonomy is 36 × 36 — what the player does (axis X) against what the world is (axis Y). Here it begins the way everything here begins: small. Two actions × two worlds = four frames. Nothing below is a game yet — each cell is only a name and a frame (~Frame: the three-block interface). Tap a cell, and the tap itself creates the game — at that moment, from that name alone. The game is saved as it is created (marked ✦) and grows one step ahead of you as you play: only the current screen and its branches ever exist.

X01 · Movement — the player moves, leaps, crosses.
X25 · Conversation — the player talks and negotiates.
Y01 · Rigid Physics — a world of falling stones and solid ground.
Y17 · Artificial Minds — a world of minds made by hands, thinking their own thoughts.

The four corners of the map: body and word, against stone and mind. A game here is composed of nothing but its two categories — the role comes from X, the matter from Y.

Part of ZenSwipe Games — games with no script, generated as you play. The full 36 × 36 taxonomy is Kirill Minin's design (2026), kept as a document and a workbook; this page is its living seed.
