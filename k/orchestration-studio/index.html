The engine: Activepieces

Our roadmap runs on Activepieces — the open-source automation platform. Self-hosted on our own infrastructure, so a client's operations never depend on a vendor's pricing page, outages, or shutdown decisions.

Intelligence plugs in where it adds value — classification, extraction, drafting, reasoning — while the workflow itself stays deterministic, auditable, and under human approval where it matters. Persistence and records live in a managed database; every run is logged, every exception is flagged for a person.

02The roadmap: the first 90 days

A studio is built in order. Validate before building, demonstrate before selling, standardize only what has already sold twice.

Days 1–15

Validate the problem

Interview 15–20 owners of small B2B service firms. Map the repetitive processes, the costly delays, the manual work — and find the problem they already want to pay to solve.

Days 16–30

Build one demonstration

A working client-intake-to-proposal workflow on Activepieces: structured extraction, human approval before external commitments, error handling, and a full execution log.

Days 31–60

Sell and deploy pilots

Two or three businesses pay for implementation. Baseline time, error rate, and cost are measured before — and the improvement after is the case study.

Days 61–90

Standardize the offer

Repeated components become templates. Support procedures and security controls documented. Monitoring becomes a standing service. If the same workflow keeps selling, the vertical narrows around it.
