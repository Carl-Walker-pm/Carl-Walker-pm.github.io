The Vietnam Index

Every region. Every city.

Fortnight keeps a chapter in every city in Vietnam — 93 cities, from the high northwest to the last streets of the Mekong Delta. Each city below has its own page and its own door into the club: find yours, and your application begins with your home town already understood.

The search itself is never limited to one city. But every search starts somewhere — and it should start where you live. The club's home port is Da Nang; the full story of the club is on the Fortnight Vietnam page.
