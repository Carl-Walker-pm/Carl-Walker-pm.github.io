Long Khánh sits in the southeast, the country's engine room, where ambition arrives by the trainload. Life here runs on acquaintance — on schools, regiments, companies and families who have known each other for decades. That is not an obstacle to finding someone; it is the raw material. Fortnight's work in Long Khánh is to search those circles with discretion, and to open exactly one right door.

Long Khánh is a Class III city of Đồng Nai province, recognised as a city in 2019. Some 171,276 people live within its 195 km². It is a city with room to breathe, where everybody is somebody's cousin and a stranger is noticed by noon — which is exactly why the search here is conducted quietly.
