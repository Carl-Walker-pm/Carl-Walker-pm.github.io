A shelf of living stories. No scripts — every game generates itself while you play, one screen at a time.

How a story game works

🖼️A picture — the situation
📖Its story — a few lines
👆Your choice — what happens next

You choose. The next screen appears, with its own situation. Screen by screen, a story unfolds that has never existed before — and will never exist the same way again.
