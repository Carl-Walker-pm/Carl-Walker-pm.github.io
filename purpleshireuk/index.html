Directions

Five directions of work — from hands-on volunteering in Washington to a Norway-registered NGO. The projects below are how each direction takes shape.

01

Local, hands-on — Washington

Real logistics close to home: volunteering with Ukraine Defense Support — packing medical supplies, fundraising for non-lethal equipment, awareness events.

02

Camera for good

The camera as an instrument: photo and video for humanitarian and refugee organizations covering displacement — plus a “proceeds support Ukraine” line in the Lucid Future shop.

03

Political voice

Speaking credibly to Americans, the Russian diaspora, and Russians back home: letters to representatives, town halls, local press.

04

MOST — Norway NGO

The umbrella: an association for the rehabilitation of war-affected Russian speakers, registered in Norway — collecting the other directions as program lines.
05

Exile mentoring

Walking with new arrivals: mentoring anti-war Russians through legal help, housing, and psychological support — things only someone who walked that road can do well.
