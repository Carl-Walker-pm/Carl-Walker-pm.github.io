About

MOST is a Norwegian association for the rehabilitation of war-affected Russian speakers.

Nobody is thinking about the rehabilitation of Russians after the war — MOST exists to change that, building programs, partnerships, and funding for recovery.

Highlights

  • Rehabilitation programs for war-affected Russian speakers
  • Partnerships with NGOs, funds, and welfare institutions
  • A long-term funding strategy — pilots first, scale later

Rehabilitation program

Five pillars, one goal: recovery with dignity. The program is built on the U.S. National Institute of Mental Health's public PTSD material and delivered by licensed professionals — MOST builds access, funding, and community around it.

  • Access and assessment — screening, referral, and practical help reaching qualified, Russian-speaking professionals.
  • Evidence-based treatment — psychotherapy (including CBT, exposure therapy, cognitive restructuring) and clinician-supervised medication.
  • Community and family support — peer groups and family education; support networks are an essential part of recovery.
  • Rebuilding daily life — routines, realistic goals, stress reduction, gradual improvement.
  • Children and youth — age-adapted tracks run with child specialists.

MOST is not a clinic: we don't diagnose or prescribe. Every clinical step is delivered by licensed professionals. Crisis helpline in Norway: 116 123 (24/7).
