MOST

Rehabilitation after the war. A bridge to recovery.

In formation — Norway

About

MOST is a Norwegian association for the rehabilitation of war-affected Russian speakers.

Nobody is thinking about the rehabilitation of Russians after the war — MOST exists to change that, building programs, partnerships, and funding for recovery.

Highlights

  • Rehabilitation programs for war-affected Russian speakers
  • Partnerships with NGOs, funds, and welfare institutions
  • A long-term funding strategy — pilots first, scale later
