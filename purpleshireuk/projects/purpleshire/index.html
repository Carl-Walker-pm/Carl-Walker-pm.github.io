About

PurpleShire is an AI ontology platform that turns unstructured information — documents, reports, emails, contracts, archives, public records — into living knowledge graphs of People, Organizations, Projects, Contracts, Events, Locations, Assets and the connections between them.

As it ingests data, the platform proposes new ontology structures. Built for NGOs and civic organizations, investigative journalism, historical research, compliance and due diligence, public sector teams, and open-data initiatives.

Long-term vision: a Civic Intelligence Platform helping people and communities understand complex realities.

Highlights

  • Entity → relationship extraction from raw documents
  • Self-proposing ontology structures as data grows
  • Knowledge-graph search and reasoning over connected facts
  • Built for civic, journalistic, and research use cases
