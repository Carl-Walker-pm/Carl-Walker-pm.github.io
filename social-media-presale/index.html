How it works

  1. Brief. An agent-led onboarding brief captures your voice, audience, and goals.
  2. Dossier. Within 72 hours you get a structured client profile — the single source of truth for everything produced.
  3. Work orders. The director turns the brief into production work orders — one order, one finished piece.
  4. Grading. Every piece is claim-graded: SHIP, REWORK, or HOLD. Only SHIP ships. Refusals are logged.
  5. Honest report. You get real metrics only. Anything unmeasured is labeled "unmeasured" — never laundered into certainty.
