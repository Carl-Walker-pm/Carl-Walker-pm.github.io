▶ The Concept

Wall Street meets the racetrack. Each episode covers one real funding cycle as a live horse race — with play-by-play commentary, slow-motion replays of term sheets, and a photo finish at the valuation line.

“They're off! Series B breaks clean from the gate… but look at the Dark Horse closing on the outside! Oh no — a down round at the quarter pole, the Favorite is fading… and it's a photo finish at the billion-dollar line!”

— RACE COMMENTATOR, EPISODE 1

▶ Episode Format

Twenty-two minutes. One race. Zero mercy.

The Starting Gate

Cold open: the racers are loaded in. Founders sweat, VCs adjust their binoculars, the commentator reads the form guide (pitch decks).

The Back Stretch

The race unfolds — term sheets fly, due diligence drags, a bridge loan makes a bold mid-race move on the rail.

The Photo Finish

Slow-motion replay at the valuation line. Was it a unicorn? The stewards (auditors) review the tape.

Winner's Circle

Trophy, champagne, and a term sheet the winner definitely should have read more carefully. Post-race interviews.

▶ The Tone

A love letter to startups with a satirist's pen. The humor comes from how seriously everyone takes the race — the commentators treat a Series A like the Olympics, and the racers behave like the money is life itself. Because for them, it is.
